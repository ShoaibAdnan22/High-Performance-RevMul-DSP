# High-Performance-RevMul-DSP

To design and implement the reversible 4x4 Urdhva Tiryakbhayam Multiplier design with minimized quantum cost and delay, which is suitable for Digital Signal Processing(DSP) applications.

The 2x2 Urdhva Tiryakbhayam multiplier uses quantum gates like Toffoli, Peres, and Feynman gates to compute the product using four equations. The 2-bit multiplier circuit has 3 garbage outputs, 5 ancilla inputs, and a quantum cost of 23 with a delay of 12δ

To efficiently generate the required product, the resulting 4x4 Vedic multiplier design includes four 2x2 multipliers, seven complete adders, one Peres gate, and one CNOT gate. While adhering to reversible logic rules, aiming to optimize quantum cost and performance.
